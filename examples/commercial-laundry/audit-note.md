## Additional checks not in the worker outputs

- **Size and timing of relief for A (stress case).** A deposit of $1,800 clears the floor only if received by the end of week 5 (received at week 6 the week-5 close is still 9,750). Because week 5 alone needs only $250, week 6 is the binding week, but a deposit must be in hand before the shortfall begins. Faster payment must be **2 weeks or faster** to survive the +2 stress: a 3-week term stressed to 5 weeks still breaches (9,750). Executor's $5,000 suggestion covers a stress of about 4 extra weeks (needs $4,900); the council's $1,800 covers exactly 2 extra weeks and leaves zero margin. Delay of +3 needs $3,350; +4 needs $4,900.
- **Receivable at week 13.** Unpaid hotel invoices at the end of week 13: A $15,750 (agreed timing) or $24,000 (stress); B $10,080 or $15,360. Reviewers 4 and 5 raised the point. Cash for weeks 14–17 under A depends on the unforecast post-week-13 existing workload and was not tested by anyone; it cannot be tested from the brief.
- **B is not free of the stress test beyond two weeks:** minimum 16,590 at +4 weeks, 16,410 at +6.
- **Earnings versus cash:** A's 13-week cash-basis result equals baseline + $10,000 only after the last receipt (week 17). By week 13 the cash difference is the receivable above.

## Conditions and the verdict

The chair's headline is conditional and correctly says A should not be signed as written, B should be sent with a conditional A, and every element (B acceptance, deposit/faster payment, exit or cap after week 13, written overtime and partner reservations) is a negotiation request. Nothing here undermines that. Material qualifications to keep beside the recommendation:

- **Relief size:** $1,800 by week 5 is the bare minimum for exactly a two-week delay; it is not a safe target. A ≥2-week payment term or a deposit of roughly $5,000 covers a four-week delay.
- **Weeks 14–26:** the 13-week analysis does not cover them; the chair's condition (exit/cap or confirmed capacity and a written partner reservation) cannot be verified from the brief.
- **Overtime confirmation** matters for B as well as A (800 lb in B's peak weeks).
- **Breakdown probability and cost of a serious service failure** are unquantified in the brief and remain unresolved.
- The verdict does not assign a probability that the hotel accepts B, as the brief requires.
