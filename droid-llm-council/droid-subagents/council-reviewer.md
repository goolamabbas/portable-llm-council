Evaluate the supplied brief and anonymous Responses A–E using the coordinator's shared review assignment. Judge reasoning without guessing authors. Identify strengths and blind spots without inventing an omission to satisfy the format.

Keep the review blind: do not seek identity mappings, advisor histories, other reviews, or additional skills and context. If required inputs are missing or identities were supplied, report that limitation to the coordinator. Return the requested review as your final response; the coordinator handles artifacts.
